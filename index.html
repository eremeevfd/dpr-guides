Evolutions F2P / non-DPS / Fresh Player

The recommended weapon evolution path if you're free-to-play or not focused on pure DPS. Don't skip ahead — each stage lists the damage multiplier and the checkpoint you need before evolving further.

  • Main line: Scythe → Shield Breaker → Sword of Light/Dark. Evolve Vanguard any time you reach Guild Tier 5.
  • Do not proceed past Vanguard Evolution 2 until it's fully achieved.
  • Use a random Base Attack piece before Vanguard is evolved — don't over-invest; 1 star is enough.

Ringlord Set

A base attack farming set, not a damage set — don't build damage stats on it.

  • 2-piece: Inactive Soul Ring pages grant 50% of their effects.
  • 4-piece: Increases Ringlord effects by 500%.
  • Ringlord Effect and All Weapon Effect are the only two main stats that matter. The latter is slightly better.
  • Use Vengeance with Ringlord to farm base attack.

Optimal Late-Game LB Setup

Thunder/Lightning-build gear: Thunder Doom (LB), Seventh Omen (M7), and 3x Ringlord (Halo), paired with the Doomstorm 4-piece set.

  • 2-piece: +3 to the maximum repeat count of Thunder Apotheosis.
  • 4-piece: Thunder Apotheosis multiplies each repeated strike's damage by x3.
  • Relic priority: x2 Critical Damage, Final Damage, Thunder Damage, Shield Damage Multiply, +1 of the above.
  • Build Thunder Damage last — but keep it if you get a lucky roll.
  • You can keep 1x Thunder Pact for early ascensions, but replace it with a Halo after.
  • If you happen to get a Halo first, just keep it.

Optimal Skull Set Setup 20M+ revives needed

Abyss Walker (Skull Coin), Weapon Sigil, Seventh Omen (M7), and 2x Ringlord (Halo), combined with the Death King 2-piece + Gravesmith 2-piece set.

  • 2-piece: Each Death King level increases Skull damage by 100%.
  • 2-piece: Converts Weapon Sigil bonuses into independent damage.
  • Relic priority: x2 Critical Damage, Final Damage, Skull Damage, Shield Damage Multiply, +1 of the above.

Skull vs. LB: Revive Count Thresholds

Which build to run depends on your revive count and whether you're farming Guild or Tower content.

  • Guild: No potions/codex → 0 revives. Crit/Insight potions + AWE/Lv. 2800 weapons → 20M–40M.
  • Tower: 20M–45M revives across every stage.
  • Why 20M–45M: These are the two thresholds where the crossover can happen, not a range you're stuck in. 20M is the earliest: it's when Skull overtakes an unbuffed LB with no potions — LB never wins even at 19M revives without them. 45M is the latest: after 45M revives, Skull out-damages LB in every situation, even a fully-potted LB running every weapon effect.

Best Relic Stats

Which main stat to chase per relic slot, and sub stat priority for each build.

  • Main stat — Core: Ringlord.
  • Main stat — Chain: All Critical Damage.
  • Main stat — Codex: All Weapon.
  • Main stat — Mask & Lantern: Attack Speed / Shadow / Critical Chance — doesn't matter which.
  • Sub stats — LB: Final Damage > x2 Critical Damage > Shield Damage > Thunder Damage. 5th piece can dupe Final Damage or x2 Critical Damage, or use Challenge HP for tower climbing.
  • Sub stats — Skull: Final Damage > x2 Critical Damage > Shield Damage > Skull Damage. 5th piece can dupe Final Damage or x2 Critical Damage, or use Challenge HP for tower climbing.

How to Upgrade Your Relics

Three ways to modify a relic: Rerolls, Resets, and Hellfire.

  • Rerolls: change every stat, redistribute stars randomly, 10% chance to add an extra star (max 5).
  • Resets: reset relic level to 0 but keep the main stat; stars are removed and re-rolled from scratch.
  • Hellfire: reduces relic level by 4, which in practice removes one latest star (needs a Devil's Horn to push 5★ → 6★).
  • 5★ and under relics should only use Resets.
  • Priority for special-type relics: Necklace > Codex > Core.

Sub-Stat Stars

Each star on a sub-stat multiplies it again, and the multiplier doubles with every star. Rarity caps how many stars a sub-stat can reach. 6★ can't be rolled: using a Devil's Horn on an Artifact relic raises its max level from 20 to 24, which unlocks one more enhancement and with it a 6th star.

Relic Set Bonuses

Equip 2 relics of the same set for the 2-piece bonus and 4 for the 4-piece bonus. Set bonuses apply whatever the relic's rarity.

Challenge HP Reduction — Is It Any Good?

Short answer: yes, but build it last.

  • 5★ relics → ~3.27x damage increase in Tower & Hell Road (≈ under 3 tower levels).
  • 6★ relics → ~144x damage increase in Tower & Hell Road (≈ 22 tower levels).
  • It should not be your first 6★ stat — other stats net a 64x damage increase (≈18 tower levels) that's also usable outside the tower, which outweighs Challenge HP Reduction even accounting for the extra ascension it grants.

Endless Abyss

  • Gain 1 Abyss Core per depth.
  • Every 5 depth: +5 Abyss Core and a Buff for the current abyss challenge.
  • The challenge fails once your HP is depleted.
  • HP works the same as in Nightmare Labyrinth: 10,000 + (Curse King Level × 20).

Pro tip: once your damage is maxed out deep in the Abyss, play manually so the Taiji effect stays on the whole time. That gets you a couple of extra depths.
Taiji is the Dual-Sword skill you unlock by evolving both the Sword of Light and the Sword of Darkness. It activates on attack for 10s (30s cooldown): attacks hit twice, and you get an independent damage bonus that stacks separately from all your other damage bonuses. Its power is (Light Lv. + 1) × (Dark Lv. + 1).

Abyss Run Rewards

  • Rewards are weekly. Check your current rank and claim last week's rewards in Weekly Rewards; your group leaderboard is on the Abyss ranking board. You stay Unranked for the week until you start an Abyss challenge.
  • You compete in a 10-player bracket, grouped by Ascension in steps of 5 (5–9, 10–14, 15–19…). You only rank against the 9 other players in your bracket.
  • Each reward has two parts. Soul Ward depends on your reward tier, which groups Ascension in steps of 20 (5–19, 20–39…). Abyss Core depends on your bracket.
  • Rank 1 Abyss Core is 100 × (the top Ascension of your bracket + 1), so 5,500 for the 50–54 bracket. Ranks 2 and 3 get 60% of that, and Ranks 4–10 get 40%.
  • Example: at Ascension 53 you compete in the 50–54 bracket. Rank 1 pays 15 Soul Ward (40–59 tier) and 5,500 Abyss Core (50–54 bracket).

Nightmare Labyrinth

  • Your HP in the labyrinth is 10,000 + (Curse King Level × 20).
  • Green doors mean every mob behind that door is cleared.
  • The white thread leads you to the last remaining enemy in the dungeon.

Labyrinth Tickets

  • Entering is free: the ticket is only spent when the boss spawns. It costs 1 ticket on Easy, Medium and Hard, and 3 on Hell.
  • You get 3 free tickets every day at 00:00 UTC.
  • You can hold up to 30 tickets. Unused tickets carry over, so you can stockpile them and do several runs in a row.
  • Buy more from:
    • Guild Shop: 2,000 Guild Coins
    • Time Shop: 10 Time Coins
    • Event Shop, when an event is active
    • Robux Shop: 99 Robux each, up to 6 per day (resets at 00:00 UTC)

Chests

Levels 1/2/3 give 2/3/5 random chests. Solving the puzzle adds one guaranteed chest: Epic on Easy, Legendary on Medium, Mythic on Hard, Artifact on Hell.

Chests can drop Gold Weapon Stones. Each one gives +1 level to the Timeforged Sword, Sword of Darkness, Shadow Sword or Midnight Harvester. See Gold Weapon Stone: SoD or TF? for where to spend them, and the Labyrinth Rewards calculator for expected drops.

Death Knight

  • The Death Knight has a 50% chance to spawn when you enter the labyrinth (up from 10% in update 12.2).
  • Killing it gives a ×3 Revive Attack buff for 2 / 4 / 6 / 8 hours on Easy / Medium / Hard / Hell.
  • You can get the Death Knight reward once per day, across all difficulties.

Soul Awakening System

  • Unlocks at Ascension 5. Found in the Soul Zone.
  • Choose one of three classes to awaken: Thunder God, Bone King or God of Time. Awakening a class costs 100 Abyss Core.
  • Soul Refines cost Abyss Core: 20 for the first refine, then more for each level after it (see table).
  • Each successful refine lowers the success rate of the next one: −5% per level up to 9→10, then −7% per level up to 15→16. After that it drops by 1% per level: 5% at 16→17, 4% at 17→18 and 3% at 18→19.
  • From 7→8 onward, a failed refine drops you 1 level.
  • From 9→10 onward, you can spend Soul Luck to double the success rate: 3 Soul Luck for 9→10 to 11→12, 4 for 12→13 to 16→17, and 5 for 17→18 and 18→19. With Soul Luck, 9→10 is a guaranteed success.
  • Max Soul Refinement level is 20.

Which Class to Awaken? Beginners

Each class does a different job, so you level all three in turn. Push each one to about Lv.10–12 (as far as your Abyss Core allows) in this order:

  1. Thunder God: Lightning damage. Early on, Skull deals less damage than LB, so everyone starts on the Lightning build. This is your fastest damage gain at low level.
  2. Bone King: Skull damage. Skull damage scales with your revive count. Somewhere around 10–20M revives it catches up with LB, and you switch to Skull: its damage is steady, while LB relies on waiting for Thunder Apotheosis (TA) procs (see Skull vs. LB thresholds).
  3. God of Time: EXP. More EXP when you claim Guild Boss rewards, so you level faster. Levels matter for Vanguard Evolution 2 and Timeforged Sword evolutions.

Gold Weapon Stone: SoD or TF?

A Gold Weapon Stone gives +1 level to the Timeforged Sword (TF), Sword of Darkness (SoD), Shadow Sword or Midnight Harvester. For damage, it comes down to SoD vs. TF: both are independent damage multipliers, so pick whichever one a level grows by more.

  • SoD: Independent Damage = Ascension × (Lv.+1) + Rebirth × (Lv.+1) × 10%. One level multiplies it by (Lv.+2)/(Lv.+1). Your Ascension and Rebirth don't change the answer. SoD maxes out at Lv.60; after that every stone goes to TF.
  • Taiji power is (Light Lv.+1) × (Dark Lv.+1), so a Dark level also grows Taiji by the same amount. While Taiji is active it dwarfs everything else, so a SoD level is worth that gain squared. Taiji duration (10/15/20s) doesn't change the pick.
  • TF: damage is (TF Level × Online Hours)power, so one level multiplies it by ((Lv.+1)/Lv.)power. Online hours don't change the answer.

XP formulas

Every XP source we've measured uses the same multiplier:

M = Rebirths × Ascension × (1 + Guild %) × (1 + Double XP + Friends + Patron + Alliance Experience Boost + Relic Exp % + Death Coins) × (1 + God of Time) × VIP 1.2 × Flood of Wisdom 1.25 × EXP Potion 1.57

Each Rebirth multiplies your XP by your Rebirth count, so Rebirth 391 earns 391/390 of Rebirth 390. Ascension multiplies by its number, as the Ascension screen shows (EXP ×71 at Ascension 71). The last group adds up: Double XP Boost is +100%, each friend in the server +10%, the Patron title +5% to +25%, the Alliance Experience Boost +5% per level (+15% at Lv.3), relics their Exp %, Death Coins their EXP % (a full set of five 60% coins is +300%). Because they add up, a +100% relic on top of Double XP and a few friends raises XP by about 40%, not ×2. The Lone Survivor title gives no XP that we can measure: two friends joining an empty server added exactly their +20%. The God of Time class multiplies on its own (the game calls it “independent” EXP): 5% at Lv.0 plus 4.75% per level, 100% at Lv.20. From Lv.4 its Shared Wisdom gives everyone else in the server 20% of that bonus; only the best one counts, and your own God of Time replaces it when it’s higher. VIP’s +20% and the Flood of Wisdom pillar’s ×1.25 also multiply on their own, and so does the EXP Potion: despite its “+100%” description it multiplies XP by 1.57, with or without relics.

Guild Boss: EXP ≈ 597K × (GB Level + 4) × M × kill bonus × (1 + Alliance Boss Reward Bonus)

You can claim 6 GB rewards per day. The kill bonus grows with each one: ×1.0, 1.1, 1.2, 1.3, 1.5 and ×2.0 for the 6th, so all six together are worth 8.1 first kills. The GB's coin reward (the +1,655 next to the level) follows 23 × (GB Level + 4).

Demon Realm: XP per 0.3s ≈ 573 × M

Once the Demon Realm boss dies, everyone in the server gets this every 0.3s until the realm closes at :50. Ticks actually land about every 0.32s, so real income is about 93% of the number the realm shows.

These formulas are fitted from our own in-game readings, not taken from the game code. Level XP matches six readings from Lv.84,729 to Lv.276,185. The GB constant is fitted to three characters (Ascension 44, 59 and 71, Rebirth 236, 324 and 390) and matches each within 1.7%; the Ascension 44 one reads about 2% more than shown, so the formula may underestimate low Ascensions. All our test characters are in one alliance (Experience Boost and Boss Reward Bonus Lv.3), so the perks are divided out of the constants by their listed %; switching relics on and off shows Experience Boost adds to the same group as Double XP. The Demon Realm constant comes from one empty-server reading, scaled to match. On a busy server expect a few percent more than shown if someone there has God of Time Lv.4+ and you don’t set it. Relic, Friend and Rebirth changes match within 0.3%. Whether the guild % multiplies separately is the least certain part. The GB Level term matches GB Lv.68–78. Death Coins, relics, the Patron title, God of Time (with Shared Wisdom), Flood of Wisdom and the EXP Potion were each switched on and off against the Boss Rewards list to find where they sit. VIP was on in every test. Hellroad isn't counted. XP fields take game-style numbers like 8.31B, 1.5T or 3e15.
